"Add content sensitivity filtering to the factoid system. This is critical for the app's tone and brand.
CHANGE 1 — UPDATE THE API PROMPT:
Add this to the factoid generation prompt, near the top of the rules section:
'CONTENT BOUNDARIES — STRICTLY FOLLOW THESE:
Do NOT generate facts about:

Slavery, slave trade, slave burial grounds, slave labor, slave markets
Genocide, ethnic cleansing, mass atrocities
Sexual assault, rape
Child abuse or child death
Lynching, racial terror
Active mass shootings (historical mob hits and crime are fine, school shootings are not)
The Holocaust (unless the fact is specifically about heroism or resistance)

These topics are historically important but this app is designed to be a fun conversation starter at bars and on dates. A fact about slavery followed by a whip crack sound effect is not the vibe. Keep it light enough that someone could read it out loud at a dinner party without the table going silent.
Crime, scandal, haunted stories, and dark history are fine — just not atrocities. The line is: would this fact make someone go "oh that's wild" (good) or "oh... that's really sad" (bad). Stay on the wild side.'

Add a content filter that runs on every fact BEFORE it is shown to the user. Check the factoid text against a blocklist of terms. If any term appears, skip that fact and show the next one instead.
Blocklist terms (case-insensitive partial match):

slave, slavery, enslaved
lynching, lynched
genocide
ethnic cleansing
sexual assault, rape, raped
child abuse, child murder
concentration camp
mass shooting, school shooting
holocaust (unless also containing: rescue, saved, resistance, survived, hero)

If a fact is filtered out, log it to console as 'FILTERED: [first 40 chars]' for debugging, and move to the next fact in the array. If ALL facts in a batch are filtered, trigger a new API call.
