# Prompt #14 — Hybrid Redesign: Direction C Energy + Existing Mechanics

"This is a major visual redesign of the Kapit app. We're keeping ALL existing functionality — the suspender snap mechanic, the API route, the spinning wheel, the factoid reveal, the repertoire — but completely reskinning the UI based on new design direction.

The new look is inspired by 'Direction C: Street' from our design explorations — jaunty, cosmopolitan, confident, works for both men and women. Think speakeasy signage meets modern streetwear. Less stuffy steakhouse, more downtown cocktail bar with great typography.

## CRITICAL: What NOT to change
- Do NOT touch the suspender snap drag/release mechanics or physics
- Do NOT touch the API route or fact generation
- Do NOT touch the spinning wheel teaser logic or timing
- Do NOT touch the sound effects implementation
- Do NOT change any business logic or state management
- Keep the circular buckle shape — do NOT make it square

## COLOR PALETTE — Light/Cream Mode (Default)
Switch the app from dark mode to a warm cream/paper background:
- Page background: #F5EDE0 (warm cream/paper)
- Card backgrounds: #FFFFFF with subtle shadow
- Primary text: #1C1916 (warm black)
- Secondary text: #5A5347 (smoke)
- Accent 1 — Bourbon: #C4793A (headlines, buckle, straps)
- Accent 2 — Powder Blue: #6A9CB7 (tags, secondary accents)
- Accent 3 — Deep Black: #1C1916 (contrast elements, badges)
- Buckle/hardware: Keep the brass/gold (#B8A44C, #D4A937)
- Suspender straps: Keep bourbon/leather (#C4793A, #2A1408)

## TYPOGRAPHY
This is the most important change. Import these Google Fonts:
- Fraunces (italic, variable weight) — for the 'Kapit' wordmark. Use ital, weight 800-900, with the SOFT axis if available
- Libre Caslon Text (italic) — for body text, taglines, factoid text
- JetBrains Mono — for small labels, metadata, coordinates, category tags

Add this to the HTML head:
<link href='https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces:ital,opsz,wght,SOFT@0,9..144,300..900,0..100;1,9..144,300..900,0..100&family=Libre+Caslon+Text:ital,wght@0,400;0,700;1,400&family=JetBrains+Mono:wght@400;500;700&display=swap' rel='stylesheet'>

## GENERAL STYLING NOTES
- Everything should feel lighter, airier, more cosmopolitan than the current dark version
- Generous whitespace — don't crowd elements
- Cards and sections should have subtle shadows (0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.06))
- Rounded corners on pills/badges (border-radius: 999px) but sharp corners on main cards (border-radius: 2-4px)
- The overall vibe is: downtown cocktail bar with great signage, not a private members club
- It should appeal equally to men and women
- The jaunty/tilted elements (wordmark, tagline badge) should be subtle — 2-3 degrees max, not dramatic"
